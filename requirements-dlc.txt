# Optional pose-estimation stack, installed by install_dlc.bat into a SEPARATE
# virtual environment (.venv-dlc).
#
# Why separate: DeepLabCut pins numpy, and historically has pinned it against
# what the rest of this app wants. Keeping it in its own environment means a DLC
# dependency conflict can never stop HTR Studio from opening and scoring a trial.
#
# Why optional at all: the shipped detector needs no trained model and scores
# F1 = 1.00 on the synthetic benchmarks. A pose model earns its keep when you
# want keypoints for something else -- grooming, rearing, locomotion -- or when
# real-mouse footage turns out to defeat blob tracking (very low contrast,
# bedding, two animals in frame).
#
# CPU-only build: this host has no CUDA GPU. Change the index URL below if you
# move to an NVIDIA machine, where training drops from hours to minutes.

--extra-index-url https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cpu
